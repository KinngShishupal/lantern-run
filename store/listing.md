## Short description (max 80)

Carry your lantern through 16 night-time stages and bring the light home.

## Full description (max 4000)

The Great Lantern on Hollow Hill has gone dark, and its light has scattered
across the land as glowing seeds. You are the Lantern Keeper. Take your little
lantern, gather the seeds, and bring the light home.

Run, jump and double-jump through four hand-painted worlds:

• Night Garden: moss, mushrooms and a very grumpy Beetle Baron
• Misty Marsh: crumbling ledges, hopping crickets and the ground-shaking Bog Toad
• Moonlit Ridge: diving bats, swarms of moths and the Moth Queen
• Ember Hollow: flame vents, thorn wheels and the blazing Ember King

Features
• 16 stages: 12 levels and 4 boss fights, each boss with its own attacks,
  a rage phase, and an arena that fights back
• A story that unfolds between worlds
• Bounce mushrooms, crumbling platforms, flame vents and spinning thorn wheels
• Stomp beetles, crickets, bats and spitting plants
• Checkpoints, a first-play tutorial, and simple touch controls
• Unlock each stage by clearing the one before it, and replay any stage you've opened
• No ads, no in-app purchases, no internet needed

Cartoon fantasy violence only: you stomp cartoon bugs and bosses, with no blood
or gore. No sexual content, drugs, gambling, profanity, user-generated content,

## Target audience

Suggested: 13 and over. If you include under-13s, Play applies the Families
policy, which needs extra declarations. It's doable (the app has no ads or
data collection), but pick the age group deliberately.

## Data safety form

- Does your app collect or share any required user data types? **No**
- Is all user data encrypted in transit? **Not applicable** (the app sends no data)
- Do you provide a way for users to request deletion? **Not applicable**; uninstalling
  the app or clearing its storage removes everything

What the app stores, only on the device: stages unlocked, which story scenes were
shown, and whether the tutorial was seen. Nothing leaves the device.
